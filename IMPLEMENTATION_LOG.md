## The Integrity Crisis (the pivotal event)

A direct verification pass found that several previously-reported "results"
were false or unsupported. This is the reason the entire integrity regime
exists. Corrections:

- ⚠️ **EXP-000 RETRACTED.** The original entry claimed "0 subject leakage across
  Train/Val/Test" and "<0.5 ms" latency. **Both false.**
  `Data/FINAL_PRETRAINING_VERIFICATION_REPORT.md` found **100% subject leakage**
  in the shipped MRL partitions and **no measured latency existed**. Logged as a
  retraction in `EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY.md`.
- ⚠️ **Data leakage fixed.** Leak-free subject-disjoint MRL split generated by
  `tools/build_subject_disjoint_splits.py` (seed 42, asserted 0 pairwise subject
  overlap) under `Data/mrl_eye/splits_subject_disjoint/`.
- ⚠️ **Phantom gate component removed.** The reliability gate had referenced a
  4th component (`tracking_confidence`/`tracking_quality`) that was never
  computed. Removed; weights renormalized to (0.45, 0.30, 0.25); a test now
  asserts exactly 3 components.
- ⚠️ **Duplicate model asset removed.** A byte-identical
  `micro_eyenet_int8.tflite` was deleted; trainer docstring corrected.
- ⚠️ **`drowsiness_detection` quarantined.** `DrowsinessDetectionDataLoader`
  now raises `RuntimeError` on use (it is a duplicate of MRL).
- ⚠️ **Figure/benchmark guards added.** `plot_paper_figures.py` refuses to run
  without measured JSON; the benchmark no longer returns fabricated metrics.

## EXP-004 — LOSO Ablation Evaluation on NTHU-DDD (V0–V4) — 2026-07-28

- 🧪 **EXP-004 executed**: full Leave-One-Subject-Out ablation sweep (variants
  V0–V4) over the entire NTHU-DDD dataset (66,521 frames, 4 subjects) via
  `evaluation/exp004_report.py --write`. Process exited 0; wall clock 24.8 min
  (20 subject-variant evaluations). Frame/drowsy counts match ground truth
  exactly (no decode drops).
- 🔒 No frozen logic touched. The orchestrator is an **additive** layer that
  imports and calls the unmodified `evaluation/loso_harness.py` helpers
  (`_roc_curve`, `_auc`, `_fix_operating_point(target_tpr=0.80)`, `_fpr_at_tpr`)
  and `FrameProcessor`; the canonical `roc` block written to
  `results/measured_results.json` is produced by the frozen `LOSOHarness` writer.
  Seed 42, video clock 30 fps, deterministic order, sklearn absent ⇒ hand-rolled
  numpy metrics (same methodology as EXP-002/003).
- 🧪 **Measured overall (FPR@matched-TPR=0.80 / ROC-AUC)**: V0 0.6241 / 0.6288,
  V1 0.6694 / 0.6170, V2 0.6244 / 0.6245, V3 0.6690 / 0.6133, V4 0.6690 / 0.6133.
- ⚠️ **NEGATIVE / NULL result for the primary hypothesis.** The reliability gate
  in isolation (V2) does not reduce FPR at matched TPR vs V0 (0.6244 vs 0.6241,
  flat); the speech-filter variants (V1/V3/V4) *raise* FPR (≈0.669). ROC-AUC
  never exceeds baseline. Reported honestly per the truth policy; it constrains
  the claims the paper may make.
- 🐞→🔒 **V4 ≡ V3 byte-identical** (score CSV md5 f8c298c8a7f521011ad9317da0b9c9b5)
  — root-caused as **structural, not a bug**: the ROC variable is
  `fusion.fatigue_score`; `FatigueFusionEngine.update(ts, reliability)`
  (`src/fatigue_fusion.py:154`) has no CNN input, and the CNN verdict is consumed
  only by `StateManager.update` (`src/state_manager.py:374–388`) to toggle the
  `should_alarm` boolean, never `fatigue_score` (`:425`). The CNN is genuinely
  active (instrumented: 89 invocations / 6,000 subject-005 frames). Its effect is
  alarm-suppression only, invisible to a frame-level fatigue-score ROC. Flagged
  as a limitation and the motivation for EXP-005 (event-level ablation).
- 📄 Artifacts: `experiments/EXP-004_loso/` (exp004_metrics.json,
  per_variant_metrics.csv, per_subject_metrics.csv, scores/V0–V4_*.csv, 4 plots),
  `results/measured_results.json` (roc block, frozen writer),
  `results/fig_roc_curves.png`, `reports/EXP-004_REPORT.md`. Pre-run backup:
  `results/measured_results.json.pre_exp004.bak`.
- 🔒 Verification after the run: `verify_integrity.py` 6/6 PASS;
